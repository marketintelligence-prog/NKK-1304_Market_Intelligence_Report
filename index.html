Synthesis & Manufacturing

Route / MaterialDetailsReference
Primary Route (2-step, multi-step organic)o-Phenylenediamine + phenyl isocyanate → 1-(2-aminophenyl)-3-phenylurea; then benzenesulfonyl chloride (base) → NKK-1304. Yield not disclosed.[S4] (citing EP 2 923 851; Perry, Synth. Commun. 38 (2008) 3354; Singh, J. Mater. Chem. C 3 (2015) 5524)
Secondary Route (reverse order)o-Phenylenediamine + benzenesulfonyl chloride → N-(2-aminophenyl)benzenesulfonamide; then phenyl isocyanate → product. Regio-selectivity of mono-sulfonylation is the key control point.MI inference from EP 2 923 851 generic formula Ar1-SO2-NH-Ar-NH-CO-NH-Ar2 [S1]
Tertiary Route (phosgene-free urea)Urea formed from the amine with a phenyl carbamate / CDI-type reagent instead of isolated phenyl isocyanate — avoids phosgene handling at the plant.MI inference — not found in a published process; validate in CRO
Crystal-form stepFinal recrystallisation to the patented crystal modification (mp 160–162 °C) gives background stability; generic makers must use a different form or license.[S5]
Key Raw Material 1 — o-PhenylenediamineCommodity aromatic diamine; Indian production exists (MI, verify suppliers)MI
Key Raw Material 2 — Phenyl isocyanateNeeds phosgenation (aniline + phosgene). Limited Indian merchant availability — import or captive phosgene. KEY ENTRY BARRIER.MI
Key Raw Material 3 — Benzenesulfonyl chlorideFrom benzene + chlorosulfonic acid; available in India (MI)MI
Manufacturing ComplexityMEDIUM — 2 steps, simple equipment, but isocyanate safety, impurity control (bis-urea, di-sulfonamide) and polymorph control matter for paper performance.[S5]
India Manufacturing NoteIndia already makes it at scale: Deccan Fine Chemicals ships ~1.4–1.7 kt/yr from Vizag to Nippon Soda (pattern of a long-term contract). A second Indian source is only viable with a non-Nippon customer and an FTO-clear crystal form.[S39][S26]

STRENGTHS

India proven as low-cost scale base (≈US$9/kg FOB); simple 2-step chemistry; our CRO + sourcing desk can serve coaters quickly.

Bisphenol bans (WA 2026, CA 2027/28, EU group restriction); India IS 17568 BPA-free; mills seeking a second source outside Nippon Soda.

Pergafast 201/425, UU developers, developer-free paper (Blue4est); China generic price pressure; captive Deccan–Nippon contract.
